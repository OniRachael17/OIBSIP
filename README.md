## Project Overview

This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of retail sales data to identify sales patterns, customer behaviour trends, and useful business insights.

The analysis covers data inspection and cleaning, descriptive statistics, customer demographics, category performance, monthly and quarterly sales trends, correlation analysis, and additional visualizations.

## Tools and Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Google Colab
* Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed

The project includes:

* Initial dataset inspection
* Data cleaning and preparation
* Missing-value analysis
* Duplicate-value checking
* Descriptive statistics
* Gender analysis
* Age-group analysis
* Sales by category
* Quantity sold by category
* Monthly sales trend
* Quarterly sales trend
* Correlation heatmap
* Distribution of transaction sales by category
* Customer spending analysis

## Key Findings

* Electronics generated the highest total sales among the available categories.
* Clothing recorded the highest quantity sold.
* Female customers recorded slightly higher total sales than male customers.
* Sales varied across months and quarters.
* Different customer age groups contributed differently to overall sales.

## Business Recommendations

1. Maintain adequate inventory for high-performing categories, particularly Electronics.
2. Use promotional strategies during periods with lower sales to improve demand.
3. Strengthen Clothing sales through suitable promotions and product combinations.
4. Use customer demographic insights to develop targeted marketing strategies.
5. Monitor high-value customers and consider loyalty or personalized offers.

## Dataset Limitation

The available dataset contains category-level information but does not contain individual product names or product IDs. Therefore, a true Top 10 Best-Selling Products analysis could not be performed without introducing information that is not present in the dataset.
